Guide to Graduate School

This guide is for IBSC seniors who want to study a master's degree without paying tuition and with money to live on.

We chose 15 master's programs that are taught in English and related to strategic communication: 5 in Taiwan, 5 in other Asian countries, and 5 in Europe. For each program we explain the funding, the requirements, and every document you need to apply. Then we rank the best 10.

What this guide offers

Programs15 master's programs (5 Taiwan, 5 Asia, 5 Europe)
Funding11 programs have a FULL PACKAGE and 4 are PARTLY FUNDED
Monthly stipendsFrom about NT$ 4,400 to about NT$ 73,300
For every programLinks, funding details, a checklist of 16 document items, deadlines, and screenshots
RankingA Top 10 Tier List with 4 weighted criteria

How to use this guide

  1. Read Start Here. It explains difficult words and gives a general checklist and timeline.
  2. Open Taiwan, Asia, and Europe. In each profile, read Who can apply first. Many scholarships are only open to some nationalities.
  3. Use Compare to see stipends, fees, tests, and deadlines in one table.
  4. Read the Top 10 Tier List to see our ranking and why.
  5. Open the official page, check every fact again, and apply before the deadline.
