How to use this guide

  1. Read Start Here to learn the words and see what to prepare and when.
  2. Open Taiwan, Asia or Europe. The box at the top of each profile shows funding, stipend, tuition, fee and deadline.
  3. In the Funding part, read Who can apply before anything else.
  4. Use Compare and the Top 10 Tier List to choose your shortlist.
  5. Open the official links, check the facts again, and apply before the deadline.

What the two badges mean

FULL PACKAGE
A 100% tuition waiver and a monthly stipend for living costs. Almost all are competitive: meeting the rules does not guarantee the money.
PARTLY FUNDED
Only part of your costs: tuition only, a capped tuition subsidy, a stipend for only part of the program, or a package that is not confirmed.
