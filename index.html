What the two badges mean

FULL PACKAGE
A 100% tuition waiver and a monthly stipend for living costs. Almost all are competitive: meeting the rules does not guarantee the money.
PARTLY FUNDED
Only part of your costs: tuition only, a stipend that does not cover tuition, a very small stipend, or money for only part of the program.

How to use this guide

  1. Start Here explains the words, the documents and the timeline.
  2. Open Taiwan, Asia or Europe. The box at the top of each profile shows funding, stipend, tuition, fee and deadline.
  3. Read Who can apply in the Funding part. Many scholarships are limited by nationality, grades or work experience.
  4. Open the official links, check the facts again, and apply before the deadline.
