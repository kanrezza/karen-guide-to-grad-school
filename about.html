Exchange rates

How we convert money: we show every amount in the local currency and in New Taiwan Dollars (NT$), rounded to the nearest NT$ 100. Rates used: EUR 1 = NT$ 36.46 (18 Oct 2026), SEK 1 = NT$ 3.26 (16 Oct 2026), HUF 1 = NT$ 0.100 (26 Oct 2026), JPY 1 = NT$ 0.205 (17 Oct 2026), SGD 1 = NT$ 25.28 (15 Oct 2026), THB 1 = NT$ 0.965 (23 Oct 2026), CNY 1 = NT$ 4.80 (3 Aug 2026).
