Criteria and method

CriterionTypeWeightWhat it measuresScale (0 to 5)
Funding coverageFinancial35%How much of tuition and living costs the scholarship pays.5 = tuition + stipend + housing; 0 = not verified
Fit with IBSCEducational35%How closely the courses continue strategic communication.5 = strategic communication; 1 = distant field
Eligibility and timingPractical20%Whether a 2027 IBSC graduate can apply and qualify.5 = open call for seniors; 0 = work experience required
Application burdenFinancial10%Fees, tests, portfolio and document work.5 = free, standard documents; 2 = portfolio or samples

Score / 100 = Funding × 7 + Fit × 7 + Access × 4 + Burden × 2

  • Order: all FULL PACKAGE programs first, then PARTLY FUNDED. Inside each group, by score.
  • Tiers: S = full package, 80+. A = full package, 65 to 79. B = full package with major limits. C = partly funded.

Two awards

Best for Funding

TNUA, Studies of Arts and Creative Industries + TaiwanICDF (conditional)

For an eligible student, tuition, housing, insurance, books and airfare plus NT$ 18,000 a month make the broadest documented package.

Best for Education

Lund University, Strategic Communication

Dedicated teaching in organisational communication, public relations and branding is the most direct continuation of the IBSC major, although the scholarship does not pay living costs.
