Top 10 Tier List

We compared all 15 programs with four criteria. Two are financial, one is educational, and one is practical.

Criteria and weights

CriterionTypeWeightWhat it measuresHow we scored it (1 to 10)Why it matters for IBSC students
Funding strengthFinancial35%Is it a full package, and how far does the stipend go in that city?10 = full package with a stipend clearly above basic living costs. 5 = tuition coverage not confirmed. 3 = stipend far below living costs.Without money to live on, you cannot study. This is the main goal of the guide, so it has the highest weight.
Course fit with IBSCEducational30%How closely the courses continue IBSC subjects: public relations, branding, campaigns, media, and research.10 = the program is strategic communication. 5 = a related field with only some communication courses.A good fit lets you build on what you learned in IBSC instead of starting a new field.
Access for a 2027 graduatePractical20%Can a senior apply this year and qualify? We look at nationality limits, work experience, degree-in-hand rules, and whether the deadline is still open.10 = open to most nationalities, no work experience, deadline still ahead. 3 = most seniors cannot qualify this year.A scholarship you cannot apply for does not help you.
Cost and effort to applyFinancial15%Application fees, extra tests such as the GRE, and unusual steps.10 = low fee and no extra tests. 5 = GRE required or several extra steps.Tests and fees cost money and time during your senior year.

Method

  1. We gave each program a score from 1 to 10 for each criterion.
  2. We multiplied each score by its weight and added them up. Then we multiplied by 10 to get a score out of 100.
    Score = (Funding × 0.35 + Fit × 0.30 + Access × 0.20 + Cost × 0.15) × 10
  3. We put all Full package programs above all Partly funded programs, even when a partly funded program had a higher score.
  4. When two programs had the same score, the one with the better course fit ranked higher.
