Criteria and method

CriterionTypeWeightWhat it measuresScale (0 to 5)
Funding coverageFinancial35%How much of tuition and living costs the scholarship pays.5 = full tuition + stipend + housing; 4 = full tuition + stipend; 3 = limited tuition or stipend duration; 2 = tuition only; 1 = small tuition discount; 0 = not verified
Fit with IBSCEducational35%How closely the courses continue strategic communication.5 = strategic communication; 1 = distant field
Eligibility and timingPractical20%Whether a 2027 IBSC graduate can apply and qualify.5 = open call for seniors; 0 = work experience required
Application burdenFinancial10%Fees, tests, portfolio and document work.5 = free, standard documents; 2 = portfolio or samples

Score / 100 = Funding × 7 + Fit × 7 + Access × 4 + Burden × 2

Taiwan uses one distinct scholarship per university. Asia programme and scholarship details follow the project research file: PKU uses BGS Type A; Yonsei uses GKS-G University Track. Dated 2026 intakes are reference rounds.

  • Order: all FULL PACKAGE programs first, then PARTLY FUNDED. Inside each group, by score; ties retain list order.
  • Tiers: S = full package, 80+. A = full package, 65 to 79. B = full package with major limits. C = partly funded.
