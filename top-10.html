Our criteria

Two criteria are financial, one is educational and one is practical. Each is scored from 0 to 5.

CriterionTypeWeightWhat it measuresHow we score it (0 to 5)Why it matters
Funding coverageFinancial35%How much of tuition, living costs and extra support the named scholarship route pays.5 = full tuition + stipend + housing; 4 = full tuition + stipend; 3 = most tuition + a reliable allowance with a small known gap; 2 = tuition only, capped tuition, or a stipend missing for part of the degree; 1 = a small discount; 0 = program funding not verified.Affordability is central, but a large stipend alone does not prove you can live on it.
Fit with IBSCEducational35%How well the program continues Yuan Ze Strategic Communication: PR, branding, organisational communication, media and communication research.5 = specialist strategic or international communication; 4 = communication, media or corporate/digital marketing is central; 3 = nearby field such as arts, design, intercultural leadership or public policy; 2 = broad business or commerce; 1 = distant field such as economics; 0 = no connection.Subject fit gets the same weight as funding: graduates should build on their communication knowledge and career plans.
Eligibility and timingPractical20%Whether a final-year IBSC student can use the entry route and application cycle.5 = route for final-year students and an upcoming call; 4 = possible with academic or experience preparation; 3 = final-year procedure, nationality route or next call needs confirming; 2 = old call closed or participation unresolved; 1 = major subject mismatch; 0 = required work experience rules out a typical senior.A strong award is of little use if you cannot qualify. We use the lowest category that applies.
Application burdenFinancial / practical10%Known fees, extra tests, portfolio work and document preparation.5 = free with standard documents; 4 = known fee of NT$ 1,500 or less and no GRE/GMAT; 3 = larger or unknown fee with standard documents; 2 = portfolio, work samples, supervisor consent or heavy verification; 1 = several extra tests; 0 = process unusable.Fees and preparation matter to seniors, but should not outweigh funding and fit. An unknown fee scores 3, never "free".

Method

Score out of 100 = Funding × 7 + Fit × 7 + Access × 4 + Burden × 2

  1. We score each program from 0 to 5 on the four criteria, using the rubric above and the team's research documents.
  2. We sort: all FULL PACKAGE programs first, then all PARTLY FUNDED programs. Inside each group: by score, then fit, then access, then university name. So a partly funded program can score higher but still rank lower.
  3. We group the top 10 into tiers. Tier S: full package, 80 or more. Tier A: full package, 65 to 79. Tier B: other full packages with major entry or fit limits. Tier C: the best partly funded programs.
Full package
The named, competitive scholarship route pays full tuition and a monthly living allowance. It does not mean the money is automatic, or that the stipend covers every city's living costs.
Partly funded
A known tuition or living-cost gap, tuition-only aid, or no confirmed full-package route. We do not combine awards that cannot be held together.
Who the ranking is for
A mixed-nationality IBSC class. No student is assumed eligible for every scholarship. Taiwan's foreign-student awards are not for Taiwanese citizens, and earlier study in Taiwan can affect eligibility.
Evidence
Scored on 31 October 2026 from our research documents plus targeted checks of official pages. Closed 2026 calls are labelled. Missing fees are not treated as free. Scores are our judgment, not admission chances.

1. NCCU, International Communication Studies (IMICS) 81/100

Funding 4: MOE + NCCU top-up with stipend. Fit 5: international communication is central. Access 3: nationality, earlier Taiwan study and the next round need confirming. Burden 3: fee not established.

2. NTU, Communication Studies by Research (WKWSCI) 80/100

Funding 4: tuition and a monthly stipend. Fit 4: broad communication research. Access 4: honours-level research preparation. Burden 4: SGD 54 fee and no GRE.

3. Aarhus, Mundus Journalism 76/100

Funding 4: tuition and a monthly stipend. Fit 4: journalism and media are central. Access 4: the experience rule can be met with eligible internships. Burden 2: work samples and proof of experience.

4. TNUA, Arts and Creative Industries (SAC) 70/100

Funding 5: tuition, stipend and housing. Fit 3: cultural and creative industries are nearby. Access 2: next-cycle participation is unresolved. Burden 3: fee not established.

5. CLIDE, Intercultural Leadership (Erasmus Mundus) 67/100

Funding 4: tuition and a monthly stipend. Fit 3: intercultural leadership is nearby. Access 3: final-year rules and 2027 dates need confirming. Burden 3: fee not established.

Funding 5: Type A tuition, housing and stipend. Fit 3: public policy is nearby. Access 0: required work experience excludes a typical senior. Burden 2: heavy qualification verification.

Funding 4: tuition and a monthly stipend, without a claim that it covers living costs. Fit 2: broad commerce. Access 2: the cited round has closed and department participation varies. Burden 3: fee above NT$ 1,500.

Corrections we made

  • The MOE tuition cap does not make MCU fully funded without a confirmed top-up.
  • NCCU's pilot scheme excludes credit fees, so the ranking uses the separate MOE route.
  • General Yonsei GKS information does not establish a full package for the Global MBA, so Yonsei is listed as Partly funded.
  • CLIDE's supplied image was a generic scholarship graphic and was replaced with a university photo.
