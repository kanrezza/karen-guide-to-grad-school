How we rank

Full packages first. Then weighted score.

40%Financial

Funding coverage

Tuition, stipend and living benefits. Makes the remaining costs visible.

35%Educational

Fit with IBSC

Communication courses and research. Builds on your IBSC degree.

15%Practical

Eligibility and timing

Degree, nationality and intake rules. Checks whether you can apply.

10%Financial + practical

Application burden

Fees and document work. Helps you plan time and cash.

Score /100 = F × 8 + E × 7 + R × 3 + B × 2. Each criterion is scored 0–5. Scholarships are competitive.

Scoring guide and method

F · Funding coverage

5
Full tuition, stipend and housing
4
Full tuition and stipend
3
Stipend with a tuition gap or limited coverage
2
Tuition award without a stipend
1
Small tuition discount
0
No award stated

E · Fit with IBSC

5
Direct strategic communication or core communication research
4
Closely related media, discourse or digital marketing
3
Adjacent field using communication skills
2
Weaker subject connection
1
Major disciplinary change
0
No clear connection

R · Eligibility and timing

5
Direct background and a clear current call
4
Honours or relevant subject credits need checking
3
Extra experience, research credits or a reported call
2
Substantial nationality or future-award confirmation
1
Closed reference round or major prior-degree mismatch
0
Post-degree work excludes a typical graduating senior

B · Application burden

5
Confirmed free and standard documents
4
Free or low fee; mostly standard online documents
3
Extra statements, separate processes or unresolved fees
2
Substantial samples, authentication or paperwork
1
Portfolio, video and prior supervisor consent
0
Process cannot be assessed

Why these weights: funding is the first constraint; educational fit makes the degree useful. Eligibility can block a route, while application costs and paperwork can usually be planned.

Order: all 9 full-package routes precede the 6 partly funded routes. Within each group, sort by total score. Ties use Fit, Funding, Eligibility, Burden, then university name.

Funding badges: the selected award must be won. A full package covers full tuition and a living stipend; admission does not guarantee funding. Scores are comparative judgments, not admission probabilities.

NCCU scores 74 but ranks 10 under the funding rule. PKU remains a later-career route because of its work requirement.
