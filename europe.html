Short description

Lund University's two-year MSc in Strategic Communication is taught at the Helsingborg campus in Sweden. It covers strategic communication together with organisational communication, public relations, and brand communication in private, public, and non-profit organisations. In the third semester, students choose elective courses, an internship, or an exchange semester. Graduates work as communication strategists, PR consultants, and communication managers.

Why it fits IBSC

This is strategic communication at master's level, taught by a department that specialises in the field. The entry rule asks for 90 credits in strategic communication or media, which IBSC graduates have.

Related courses:

  • Strategic communication theory
  • Brand communication
  • Internship course (KOMN03)

Short description

Mundus Journalism is a two-year Erasmus Mundus joint master's program. Students spend the first year at Aarhus University in Denmark and study journalism, media, and globalisation. In the second year they choose a specialisation: Politics and Communication in Amsterdam, Crisis and Conflict in London, Cultures and Contexts in Munich, or Authoritarianism and Transition in Prague. Classmates come from more than 100 countries.

Why it fits IBSC

The Politics and Communication track in Amsterdam is close to political and strategic communication. You also get a joint European degree and a large international alumni network.

Related courses:

  • Politics & Communication (Amsterdam track)
  • Journalism and globalisation (Aarhus)
  • Crisis & Conflict (London track)

Short description

Euroculture is a two-year Erasmus Mundus joint master's program coordinated by the University of Groningen. It studies society, politics, and culture in Europe in a global context. Students study at two or more partner universities, such as Göttingen, Uppsala, or Kraków, and choose a research or professional track. Partners in Japan, India, Canada, and Mexico offer extra options. The program ends with a thesis.

Why it fits IBSC

Euroculture is useful for IBSC students interested in EU institutions, cultural diplomacy, or international NGOs. It adds European policy knowledge to communication skills.

Related courses:

  • European culture and identity
  • Professional track / internship
  • Research seminar and thesis

Short description

GLOCAL (Global Markets, Local Creativities) is an Erasmus Mundus master's program coordinated by the University of Glasgow with six partner universities. It studies how global markets meet local economies, including creative and cultural industries, entrepreneurship, and business history. Students follow one study track at two or three universities and receive a joint or multiple degree. Partners include Barcelona, Rotterdam, Uppsala, Göttingen, Kyoto, and Bogotá.

Why it fits IBSC

GLOCAL suits IBSC students interested in creative industries, place branding, and international business. It explains the markets that strategic communicators work in.

Related courses:

  • Institutional Change and Creative Industries (track)
  • Global markets and development
  • Entrepreneurship and local economies

Short description

The MA in Communication and Media Studies at Corvinus University of Budapest is a two-year program taught in English. It covers interpersonal, social, strategic, and new media communication. Students learn to manage media and plan creative campaigns, and they complete a 90-hour internship. Graduates work in public relations, media, and marketing. Budapest has lower living costs than most EU capital cities.

Why it fits IBSC

The course list reads like an advanced IBSC: persuasion, public relations, marketing communication, and media strategy. It is a practical choice for agency or in-house communication jobs.

Related courses:

  • Persuasive Communication and Rhetoric
  • Public Relations and Marketing Communication Design
  • Media Strategy
