Erasmus Mundus Scholarship, Mundus Journalism Full package

Funded by European Union, through the Mundus Journalism consortium.

Tuition support
100% for 2 years (NTD 757,575 for non-EU students).
Monthly stipend
NTD 50,505 per month for up to 24 months. Health and liability insurance included.
Who can apply
  • All nationalities, including Taiwanese applicants, subject to admission rules.
  • Previous Erasmus Mundus scholarship recipients are ineligible.
How to apply
One application for admission and scholarship, by 10 January 2027, 13:00 CET.
Selection and renewal
Highly competitive: about 10–15 awards for 2027–2029. Year-two living-cost coverage is not verified.

Axel Adler Scholarship 2027 Partial

Funded by University of Gothenburg.

Tuition support
100% of the NTD 976,080 tuition for selected students.
Monthly stipend
None. No living or travel allowance.
Additional benefits
Fee-paying students are covered by the university's FAS+ insurance.
Who can apply
International applicants who must pay tuition fees.
How to apply
  • Rank Gothenburg first; apply by 15 January 2027.
  • Pay the fee and submit documents by 1 February.
  • Eligible applicants receive scholarship instructions around 10 February.
Selection and renewal
Competitive: usually one award per program, sometimes more. Selection mainly uses your CV and motivation letter.
Other scholarships
SI funding covers tuition and NTD 39,840/month, but Taiwanese-only applicants are ineligible under its 34-country list.

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: Total tuition: NTD 896,400.

Lund University Global Scholarship Partial

Funded by Lund University.

Tuition support
Part or all of NTD 896,400. Coverage depends on the faculty budget, not your grades.
Monthly stipend
None. You must show you can fund living costs for your residence application.
Who can apply
Tuition-paying citizens outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland, including Taiwanese applicants. Rank Lund first.
How to apply
  • Complete admission and fee payment first.
  • Apply in the scholarship portal with a motivation letter (max 600 words).
  • The portal usually opens in early February. The 2026 deadline was 16 February; the 2027 date is unconfirmed.
Selection and renewal
  • Highly competitive.
  • Selection considers grades, fit, commitment and first-choice ranking.
  • Financial need is not considered.
Other scholarships
SI tuition-and-living support is unavailable to Taiwanese-only applicants.

Source: Lund University Global Scholarship
