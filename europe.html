Axel Adler Scholarship 2027 Partial

Source
University of Gothenburg.
Tuition covered
100% of the SEK 294,000 tuition for selected students.
Monthly stipend
None. No living or travel allowance.
Extra benefits
Fee-paying students are covered by the university's FAS+ insurance.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Competitive: usually one award per program, sometimes more. Selection mainly uses your CV and motivation letter.
Separate application?
Yes. Rank Gothenburg first and apply by 15 January 2027. Pay the fee and submit documents by 1 February; eligible applicants receive scholarship instructions around 10 February.
Who can apply
International applicants who must pay tuition fees.
Other funding
SI funding covers tuition and SEK 12,000/month, but Taiwanese-only applicants are ineligible under its 34-country list.

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: Total tuition: SEK 270,000 (about NT$ 896,300; SEK 1 = NT$ 3.32 on 31 Oct 2026).

Lund University Global Scholarship Partial

Source
Lund University.
Tuition covered
Part or all of SEK 270,000. Coverage depends on the faculty budget, not your grades.
Monthly stipend
None. You must show you can fund living costs for your residence application.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Highly competitive. Selection considers grades, fit, commitment and first-choice ranking; financial need is not considered.
Separate application?
Yes. Complete admission and fee payment, then apply in the scholarship portal with a motivation letter (max 600 words). It usually opens in early February; 2026 closed on 16 February, but the 2027 date is unconfirmed.
Who can apply
Tuition-paying citizens outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland, including Taiwanese applicants. Rank Lund first.
Other funding
SI tuition-and-living support is unavailable to Taiwanese-only applicants.

Source: Lund University Global Scholarship
