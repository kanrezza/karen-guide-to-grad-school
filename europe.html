2Short description

The Erasmus Mundus Master's in Journalism, Media and Globalisation is a two-year, English-taught program that combines journalism, media studies, and global communication. Students spend year one at Aarhus University and year two at a European partner, specialising in politics, communication, or crisis and conflict. It builds research, storytelling, and analytical skills, with professional placements.

3Why it fits IBSC

The program builds on IBSC knowledge of communication theory, media production, and international communication. It also develops cross-cultural perspective through study in two European countries.

Related courses

  • Introduction to Communication Theory (IBSC)
  • Journalistic English (IBSC)
  • Intercultural Communication (IBSC)

2Short description

CLIDE is a two-year, 120-ECTS Erasmus Mundus Joint Master that prepares leaders for multicultural organisations going through digital change. Five universities in Europe and North Africa offer two tracks: Leadership in the Digital Era, and Leading Intercultural Teams. Coursework, intercultural exchanges, placements, and a dissertation build skills for managing international teams.

3Why it fits IBSC

CLIDE builds on IBSC's intercultural communication, strategic communication, and digital media. It focuses more on organisational leadership than on strategic communication, but it develops cross-cultural management and communication skills.

Related courses

  • Intercultural Communication (IBSC)
  • Seminar in Public Relations and Strategic Communication (IBSC)
  • Multimedia Content Management and Creation with AI (IBSC)

The Master in Digital Communication is a two-year, 120-ECTS program in digital and organisational communication. Students explore communication theory, social media, digital rhetoric, strategic communication, and research methods, and specialise in digital or organisational communication. Electives, exchange, or an internship come before an independent master's degree project.

2Short description

The MSc in Strategic Communication at Lund University is a two-year interdisciplinary program that prepares students for strategic communication roles in international organisations. It covers organisational communication, public relations, brand communication, and stakeholder relationships in private, public, and non-profit sectors. Electives, internships, and exchange let students shape their own path.

3Why it fits IBSC

Strategic communication is the core of this program, which makes it a direct continuation of IBSC. Its focus on organisational communication, stakeholders, public relations, and brands strengthens strategic planning in international settings.

Related courses

  • Intercultural Communication (IBSC)
  • Seminar in Public Relations and Strategic Communication (IBSC)
  • Multimedia Content Management and Creation with AI (IBSC)

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: Total tuition: SEK 270,000 (about NT$ 896,300; SEK 1 = NT$ 3.32 on 31 Oct 2026).

Lund University Global Scholarship Partial

Source
Lund University.
Tuition covered
Part or all of the SEK 270,000 tuition. The share depends on the faculty budget, not on your grades.
Monthly stipend
None. You must show the Swedish Migration Agency that you can pay your own living costs.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Highly competitive and merit-based (grades, fit, commitment to Lund, first-choice ranking). Financial need is not considered.
Separate application?
Yes. After a complete program application and fee, apply in the scholarship portal. It usually opens in early February; the 2026 deadline was 16 February, and the 2027 date is not confirmed. Includes a scholarship motivation letter of max 600 words.
Who can apply
Citizens of countries outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland who must pay tuition and rank a Lund program first. This includes Taiwanese applicants.
Other funding
The SI Scholarship for Global Professionals covers tuition and living costs, but not for applicants with only Taiwanese citizenship.

Source: Lund University Global Scholarship

2Short description

This two-year, English-taught program combines research and applied learning in organisational communication, stakeholder relationships, digital marketing, and business strategy. Students study how organisations plan communication, engage customers, and measure digital communication. The 120-ECTS curriculum includes a supervised thesis and teaching from industry practitioners.

3Why it fits IBSC

IBSC coursework in communication, marketing, media, and social science connects directly to digital stakeholder engagement and corporate communication. Important: with a general humanities or social science degree you need at least 25 ECTS (or 15% of your credits) in marketing or corporate communication on your transcript.

Related courses

  • Corporate communication
  • Customer and stakeholder relationship management
