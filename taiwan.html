4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: Tuition NT$ 65,000 per semester. With health insurance, books, dormitory and living costs, about NT$ 148,500 per semester.

TaiwanICDF Scholarship Full package

Funded by Taiwan International Cooperation and Development Fund.

Tuition support
100% tuition and credit fees, dormitory, insurance and approved books.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 18,000, plus economy travel to and from Taiwan. Master’s funding lasts up to 2 years.
Who can apply
Citizens of eligible ICDF countries. The Cultural and Creative Industries major must appear on the 2027 program list, still pending. A Yuan Ze degree does not automatically qualify you.
Prior funding
Prior MOE, MOFA or ICDF recipients face a graduation-gap rule. Check the 2027 guide before planning direct progression from Yuan Ze.
How to apply
Apply to ICDF and TNUA separately. ICDF: 1 Dec 2026–15 Mar 2027; follow your Taiwan office’s submission rules.
Selection and renewal
Competitive; Taiwan-office recommendation and ICDF review are required. Admission does not guarantee the award. Benefits shown follow the 2026 guide; confirm the 2027 terms.

Sources: ICDF eligibility and application guides, SAC scholarship information.

2What you’ll study

Study international affairs through politics, trade, negotiation and culture. Career paths include diplomacy, NGOs and international business.

3Your IBSC connection

Builds on IBSC’s English communication and social science background, with a focus on diplomacy and global affairs.

Course connections

  • Introduction to Sociology
  • Introduction to Marketing

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: About NT$ 98,700 a year (International College master's programs: NT$ 90,600 to 106,800, adjusted every year).

MOE Taiwan Scholarship Full package

Funded by Ministry of Education, with MCU’s published tuition top-up.

Tuition support
MOE pays up to NT$ 40,000 per semester; MCU covers the remaining tuition and miscellaneous fees under its published rule. The 2026–27 top-up is confirmed; 2027–28 is pending.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 20,000 for up to 2 years. Housing, insurance and personal costs are separate.
Who can apply
Eligible foreign applicants entering a higher degree, including graduating students under MOE and home-mission rules. Excludes Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau. No concurrent scholarship.
How to apply
Apply separately for GSIA admission and the MOE award through the Taiwan office responsible for your nationality.
Selection and renewal
Competitive; local quotas apply. Graduate recipients must maintain an average of at least 80 and follow MOE/MCU rules.

Sources: MCU Taiwan Scholarship rules, Article 4, MOE Taiwan Scholarship rules.

2What you’ll study

Combine design and technology through interactive media, UI/UX and programming. Graduation requires 36 credits and a thesis.

3Your IBSC connection

An option for IBSC students who want to apply communication skills to user-centred digital design.

Course connections

  • Design Computer Graphics
  • AI Multimedia Content Management and Creation

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: About NT$ 63,002 per semester.

MOFA Taiwan Scholarship Partial

Funded by Ministry of Foreign Affairs; NTUT provides the published 50% tuition waiver.

Tuition support
50% tuition waiver. You pay the balance and other costs from your stipend or savings.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 33,000 for up to 2 years, plus economy travel to and from Taiwan.
Who can apply
Nationality restricted: mainly citizens of Taiwan’s diplomatic allies; other countries require special consideration by MOFA/the responsible Taiwan office. Do not assume Vietnamese applicants qualify.
Previous study in Taiwan
A Yuan Ze bachelor’s graduate may seek a first master’s. Previous Taiwan study at the same degree level is disqualifying. No concurrent government or university scholarship.
How to apply
Apply for NTUT admission and separately through your Taiwan embassy/representative office. Confirm that your nationality has a 2027 MOFA call.
Selection and renewal
Competitive; mission quotas and academic review apply. The stipend does not make the remaining tuition free.

Sources: NTUT master’s scholarship table, MOFA benefits and eligibility.

2What you’ll study

Study international media, digital communication and marketing. Build research skills for PR, media and international communication careers.

3Your IBSC connection

A close fit for IBSC’s communication theory, cross-cultural communication and social marketing courses.

Course connections

  • Cross-Cultural Communication
  • Communication Theories
  • International Media Advocacy and Social Marketing

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: International students pay NT$ 24,820 per semester plus NT$ 3,310 per credit (32 credits). That is about NT$ 102,600 a year.

New Inbound International Students Scholarship Pilot Plan — full award Partial

Funded by NCCU and its colleges.

Tuition support
Tuition waiver; credit fees are excluded. Insurance, accommodation and information fees are also your costs.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 10,000 for up to 2 years, plus actual round-trip airfare reimbursement of up to NT$ 30,000 per year. The travel limit is not extra monthly cash.
Who can apply
New international degree students, including eligible foreign graduates of Yuan Ze. No other full scholarship or tuition waiver.
How to apply
Apply for IMICS admission. The department/college nominates candidates during admission review; there is no separate individual scholarship application.
Selection and renewal
Competitive, with limited places. This profile shows the full award. Maintain a graduate average of at least 80, or any higher program requirement.

Sources: 2026 award notice, Pilot Plan rules.
