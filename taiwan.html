2What you’ll study

Study international media, digital communication and marketing. Build research skills for PR, media and international communication careers.

3Your IBSC connection

A close fit for IBSC’s communication theory, cross-cultural communication and social marketing courses.

Course connections

  • Cross-Cultural Communication
  • Communication Theories
  • International Media Advocacy and Social Marketing

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: International students pay NT$ 24,820 per semester plus NT$ 3,310 per credit (32 credits). That is about NT$ 102,600 a year.

MOE Taiwan Scholarship (best option) Full package

Funded by Ministry of Education, Taiwan; apply through the Taiwan office in your home country.

Tuition support
  • MOE pays up to NT$ 40,000 per semester.
  • NCCU covers the remaining tuition and credit fees for programs other than IMBA/MBA.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 20,000 for up to 2 years. Budget separately for housing, insurance and personal costs.
Who can apply
Eligible foreign students under MOE and local Taiwan-office rules. No concurrent scholarship.
How to apply
Apply separately through the Taiwan office, usually February to March.
Selection and renewal
Competitive, with local quotas and academic selection.

Source: NCCU scholarships for foreign degree students

Other funding options (3)

New Inbound International Students Scholarship Pilot Plan Partial

Funded by NCCU and its colleges.

Tuition support
  • Full scholarship: tuition and fees waived, but not credit fees.
  • Partial scholarship: tuition and fees waived, no stipend.
Monthly stipend
Full scholarship: NT$ 10,000, plus up to NT$ 30,000 a year for a round-trip flight (maximum NT$ 150,000 a year).
Who can apply
International students newly admitted this year who have no other scholarship. Up to 2 years.
Selection and renewal
Competitive. Departments or colleges recommend students.

Source: NCCU Pilot Plan announcement

New International Student Tuition Waiver Partial

Tuition support
Full tuition and credit fees, for up to 2 years.
Monthly stipend
None.
Selection and renewal
Limited places. Each college nominates students; you cannot apply on your own.

Source: NCCU scholarships for foreign degree students

NCCU Academic Excellence Scholarship Partial

Past one-time awards
  • Changes every time.
  • In spring 2026 some students received NT$ 40,000 or NT$ 50,000 once.
  • No monthly stipend and no tuition waiver.
Who can apply
  • International degree students after their first semester at NCCU.
  • Applications usually open in September and in late February to March.

Source: Regulations (PDF)

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: Tuition NT$ 65,000 per semester. With health insurance, books, dormitory and living costs, about NT$ 148,500 per semester.

Taiwan ICDF Scholarship (best option) Full package

Funded by Taiwan International Cooperation and Development Fund (ICDF) with partner universities.

Tuition support
Tuition and credit fees, plus insurance and approved book costs.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 18,000 for master's students, plus housing during your studies and a round-trip economy flight.
Who can apply
Eligible ICDF-country citizens only. Taiwanese citizens are ineligible.
How to apply
1 December 2026 to 15 March 2027. Confirm SAC is on the 2027 ICDF program list.
Selection and renewal
  • Competitive, for up to 2 years.
  • Requires Taiwan-office recommendation and scholarship review; admission does not guarantee funding.

Source: Taiwan ICDF scholarship

Other funding options (2)

MOE Taiwan Scholarship Partial

Funded by Ministry of Education, Taiwan. You apply through the Taiwan office in your home country.

Tuition support
  • Up to NT$ 40,000 per semester.
  • TNUA tuition is NT$ 65,000 per semester, so NT$ 25,000 per semester is left to pay, plus fees, insurance and housing.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 20,000 for master's students, for up to 2 years.
Who can apply
  • Foreign nationals, excluding Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau.
  • Master’s applicants usually need GPA 3.5/4.5 or above.
  • No concurrent government scholarship.
How to apply
Apply separately to the Taiwan office, usually 1 February to 31 March. University admission is also required.
Selection and renewal
Competitive. Local quotas; selection considers grades, references, language and interviews.

Source: MOE Taiwan Scholarship rules

MOE Taiwan Scholarship Partial

Funded by Ministry of Education, Taiwan. You apply through the Taiwan office in your home country.

Tuition support
  • Up to NT$ 40,000 per semester, against tuition of about NT$ 49,350.
  • You pay about NT$ 9,350 per semester; this is partly funded.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 20,000 for master's students, for up to 2 years.
Who can apply
  • Foreign nationals, excluding Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau.
  • Master’s applicants usually need GPA 3.5/4.5 or above.
  • No concurrent government scholarship.
How to apply
Apply separately to the Taiwan office, usually 1 February to 31 March. University admission is also required.
Selection and renewal
Competitive. Local quotas; selection considers grades, references, language and interviews.

Source: MOE Taiwan Scholarship rules

Other funding options (2)

Living and Learning Assistantship for International Students Partial

Funded by Ming Chuan University international student scholarship budget.

Tuition support
None.
Semester allowance
NT$ 8,000 per semester, up to NT$ 16,000 a year.
Who can apply
Enrolled international students. Not for students who hold a government scholarship or another full scholarship.
How to apply
Apply in the MCU student system: 1 to 15 October (fall) and 1 to 15 March (spring).
Selection and renewal
  • Not guaranteed.
  • You need an average grade of 80 and a conduct grade of 80 in the previous semester.
  • New students do not apply for their first semester.

Source: MCU scholarships

MCU Graduate Scholarship Partial

Department award value
Set by each department. No fixed monthly amount and no promise of a tuition waiver.
Selection and renewal
  • Competitive.
  • Needs an average grade of 70 and conduct grade of 80.
  • Apply to your department within 2 weeks of the start of each semester, in your first and second year.

Source: MCU graduate scholarships

NTUT Excellent International Postgraduate Scholarship Partial

Funded by NTUT internal scholarship funds.

Tuition support
Full tuition and fees. Not dormitory, books or insurance.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 6,000/month in year 1; none in year 2. Extra living funds are needed.
Who can apply
Eligible international degree students. No concurrent Taiwan-government/NTUT scholarship or full-time employment.
How to apply
New students apply together with the admission application.
Selection and renewal
Competitive. Reapply in year 2 with a first-year average of 80.

50% of tuition and fees for the year. No stipend.
