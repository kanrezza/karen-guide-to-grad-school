International Master's Program in International Communication Studies (IMICS)

National Chengchi University (NCCU)

FULL PACKAGE

Basic information

UniversityNational Chengchi University (NCCU)
ProgramInternational Master's Program in International Communication Studies (IMICS)
DegreeMA
Length2 years (32 credits + thesis)
City and countryTaipei, Taiwan
Taught in EnglishThe curriculum is taught entirely in English (IMICS ‘About’ page).
LinksProgram page · University website

Short description

IMICS is the English-taught master's program in communication at National Chengchi University in Taipei. It started in 2006. The program studies international and cross-cultural communication, with extra focus on the Asia-Pacific region. Teachers come from NCCU's Journalism, Advertising, and Radio & Television departments. Students take two required courses, choose electives, and write a master's thesis. A dual-degree option with the University of South Carolina is also available.

Why it fits IBSC

IMICS is the closest Taiwan match to IBSC: the same field, taught in English, with a public relations and advertising track. You can stay in Taiwan and keep building your IBSC network.

Related courses:

  • Communication Theory (required)
  • Transnational Public Relations and Advertisement (track)
  • Political Communication (track)

Separate scholarship application?Yes. You apply to NCCU and, separately, to the Taiwan office in your home country.

Other fundingOther routes: MOFA Taiwan Scholarship (diplomatic allies only), NCCU Tuition Waiver (tuition only).

Short description

GCIT is a newer English-taught master's program at NCCU. It began in 2022 and uses teachers from five colleges: Communication, Informatics, Law, Liberal Arts, and Innovation. The program connects global communication with new media technology. Course areas include new media, international journalism, human-computer interaction, marketing technology, and global communication culture. Students can also practice in the program's own new media studio.

Why it fits IBSC

GCIT suits IBSC students who liked the digital side of strategic communication, such as social media, campaigns, and data. It adds technology skills that many communication graduates do not have.

Related courses:

  • Marketing technology
  • New media
  • International journalism

LinksProgram page · University website

Short description

IMPIS is NCCU's English-taught master's program in international studies, run by the College of International Affairs since 2012. It looks at international relations from political, economic, and social points of view. Students choose a track in International Cooperation and Development or International Strategic Management. All courses are in English, and the master's thesis must also be written in English. Many students learn Mandarin at the same time.

Why it fits IBSC

IMPIS fits IBSC students who want to work in public diplomacy, NGOs, or government communication. It teaches the policy knowledge behind messages between countries.

Related courses:

  • International Strategic Management track
  • International Cooperation & Development track
  • Research methods and thesis

Short description

The Institute of Creative Industries Design at National Cheng Kung University trains people for creative industries such as advertising, media, design, festivals, and branding. All classes are taught in English. Students join one of three groups: Brand and Marketing Planning, Media and Interaction Design, or Industry and Service Innovation. Projects often use real places and events in Tainan, and students finish with a thesis.

Why it fits IBSC

IBSC students who enjoyed branding and campaign planning will know the topics in the Brand and Marketing Planning group. The program adds design thinking to your communication skills.

Related courses:

Short description

This international master's program belongs to the Department of Visual Communication Design at Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology in Tainan. It covers graphic design, interaction design, animation, and brand identity design. The program mixes studio work with research, and students finish with a thesis. STUST is a technology university, and living costs in Tainan are lower than in Taipei.

Why it fits IBSC

IBSC graduates who designed campaign visuals or social media content can turn that skill into a specialty. Visual identity is a key part of brand communication.
