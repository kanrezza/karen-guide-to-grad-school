2Short description

The IMICS program at National Chengchi University focuses on international communication, media, and cross-cultural studies. Students explore global media, digital communication, and marketing strategies while developing critical thinking and research skills. The program prepares graduates for careers in international marketing, public relations, media, and communication.

3Why it fits IBSC

IMICS suits IBSC students because it combines English proficiency with communication and social science knowledge. It helps students develop cross-cultural communication, critical thinking, and research skills for future international careers.

Related courses

  • Cross-Cultural Communication
  • Communication Theories
  • International Media Advocacy and Social Marketing

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: International students pay NT$ 24,820 per semester plus NT$ 3,310 per credit (32 credits). That is about NT$ 102,600 a year.

MOE Taiwan Scholarship (best option) Full package

Source
Ministry of Education, Taiwan. Applications go through the Taiwan office in your home country.
Tuition covered
Up to NT$ 40,000 per semester. For programs other than IMBA/MBA, NCCU covers the remaining tuition and credit fees, so tuition is fully covered.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 20,000, for up to 2 years. You still pay for housing, insurance and personal costs.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Competitive. Each Taiwan office abroad has a quota and chooses by academic performance.
Separate application?
Yes. Apply to the Taiwan office abroad, separately from your NCCU application, usually February to March.
Who can apply
Foreign students who meet MOE rules and the rules of their local Taiwan office. You cannot receive another scholarship at the same time.

Source: NCCU scholarships for foreign degree students

2Short description

The program's mission is to cultivate international talent in a fully English-taught environment for local and international students. The curriculum covers politics, society, economics, trade, communication, negotiation, and culture. Faculty with global experience organize international events, and graduates work in diplomacy, academia, business, NGOs, technology, finance, and services.

3Why it fits IBSC

IBSC students already have strong English, critical thinking, and an understanding of social and cultural issues. This program lets them go further into international relations, diplomacy, and global affairs, and prepares them for careers in international organizations, government, or global business.

Related courses

  • Introduction to Sociology
  • Introduction to Marketing

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: About NT$ 98,700 a year (International College master's programs: NT$ 90,600 to 106,800, adjusted every year).

MOE Taiwan Scholarship Partial

Source
Ministry of Education, Taiwan. You apply through the Taiwan office in your home country.
Tuition covered
Up to NT$ 40,000 per semester. MCU tuition is about NT$ 49,350 per semester, so about NT$ 9,350 per semester is not covered. The team's notes call this a full subsidy; we list it as partial for that reason.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 20,000 for master's students, for up to 2 years.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Competitive. Each Taiwan office abroad has its own quota and chooses by grades, recommendations, language and interview.
Separate application?
Yes. Apply to the Taiwan office abroad, usually 1 February to 31 March. A scholarship does not give you admission, so apply to the university as well.
Who can apply
Foreign nationals (not Mainland China, Hong Kong or Macau). Master's applicants usually need a GPA of 3.5 out of 4.5 or higher. You cannot hold another government scholarship at the same time.

Source: MOE Taiwan Scholarship rules

Living and Learning Assistantship for International Students Partial

Source
Ming Chuan University international student scholarship budget.
Tuition covered
None.
Amount
NT$ 8,000 per semester, up to NT$ 16,000 a year.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Not guaranteed. You need an average grade of 80 and a conduct grade of 80 in the previous semester. New students do not apply for their first semester.
Separate application?
Yes, in the MCU student system: 1 to 15 October (fall) and 1 to 15 March (spring).
Who can apply
Enrolled international students. Not for students who hold a government scholarship or another full scholarship.

Source: MCU scholarships

MCU Graduate Scholarship Partial

Set by each department. No fixed monthly amount and no promise of a tuition waiver.
