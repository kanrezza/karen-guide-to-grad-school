Part 2 Short description

ITASIA is an English-language graduate program at the University of Tokyo's Graduate School of Interdisciplinary Information Studies. It studies how media, information technology, and society shape each other in Asia. Classes mix media studies, sociology, and technology policy. Applicants must send a research proposal, a writing sample, and GRE scores. Students finish the two-year program with a master's thesis.

Part 3 Why it fits IBSC

ITASIA is for IBSC students who want to research digital media and public opinion in Asia. It is a strong path to a PhD or to research jobs.

Related courses

  • Media and society in Asia
  • Information technology policy
  • Research methods

Part 4 Funding

Monthly stipend
JPY 144,000 per month (about NT$ 29,500; JPY 1 = NT$ 0.205, rate on 17 Oct 2026)
Tuition covered
100% (admission, exam and tuition fees waived)
Source of the money
Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship
Guaranteed or competitive?
Very competitive.
Separate scholarship application?
Yes. MEXT ‘Embassy Recommendation’ through the Japanese embassy in your country. The round for 2027 entry closed around May–June 2026, so a current senior's realistic target is the 2028 round (opens spring 2027).
Who can apply
Citizens of countries with diplomatic ties to Japan. Applicants already living in Japan cannot use most MEXT routes.
Other funding
Without MEXT you must self-fund; tuition waivers are competitive.

Part 2 Short description

This is a research master's degree at the Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information, Nanyang Technological University. Students take three courses and write an original thesis. Research areas include marketing communication and public relations, health communication, political communication, and journalism. Students pass a qualifying exam after 12 months and submit the thesis by the end of the second year.

Part 3 Why it fits IBSC

The school covers marketing communication and public relations, two core IBSC areas, at research level. It is a good choice if you liked your IBSC research methods courses.

Related courses

Part 2 Short description

Global Business Journalism is a two-year master's program at Tsinghua University's School of Journalism and Communication. It is a partnership with the International Center for Journalists and Bloomberg News. Students learn to report on business, finance, and the Chinese economy in print, audio, video, and digital media. They use Bloomberg terminals in class. International students also take Chinese language classes.

Part 3 Why it fits IBSC

Corporate and financial communication is a large part of strategic communication. GBJ shows you how business news is made, which helps in investor relations, corporate PR, and media relations.

Related courses

  • Business and financial reporting
  • Multimedia storytelling
  • Chinese economy

Communication University of China is China's leading university for broadcasting and media. Its English-taught master's program in International Journalism and Communication trains media professionals with a global view and a strategic mindset. Courses cover communication theory, new media, journalistic writing, photography, and video production. Teaching and thesis writing are in English, and students must pass an oral thesis defense.

Part 3 Why it fits IBSC

The program aims to build a strategic mindset, the same goal as IBSC, and adds hands-on media production. It suits students who want to work in international media.

Part 2 Short description

The Master of Arts in Strategic Communication Management is an international program in the Faculty of Communication Arts at Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok. It focuses on integrated marketing communication, strategic brand management, and global communication management. Students choose Plan A (thesis) or Plan B (professional project). The program also includes study tours and exchange options with more than 50 partner universities.

Part 3 Why it fits IBSC

The program name is the same field as IBSC: strategic communication. Its courses continue directly from IBSC classes in public relations, branding, and crisis communication.

Related courses

  • Strategic Planning and Evaluation for Integrated Communications
  • Crisis Communication Management
  • Principles of Communication Management
