2Short description

This program provides advanced graduate training and research in Economics, designed to be completed in two years (maximum three years). Unlike taught Master of Economics programs, it has a substantial independent research component alongside compulsory core coursework. Applicants need a strong undergraduate honours degree in economics or a quantitative field.

3Why it fits IBSC

Building on the strategic messaging, public relations, and media analytics foundation of IBSC at Yuan Ze University, this program teaches advanced quantitative methods for analyzing digital communication. IBSC graduates can apply economic modeling to text data, consumer decisions in digital markets, and platform competition.

Related courses

  • EC5323 Text Analysis for Economists (NLP, topic models and LLMs in Python)
  • EC5322 Industrial Organisation (market structure and competitive strategy)
  • EC5319 Experimental Economics (decision-making and consumer behavior)

2Short description

The Master of Arts in International Commerce at SNU GSIS is a fully English-taught program covering global trade, international finance, multinational investment, and commercial law. Through case studies, expert seminars, and negotiation simulations, it gives students the analytical and strategic skills to work as international commerce experts in a changing global economy.

3Why it fits IBSC

Building on IBSC's strategic messaging, cross-cultural communication, and media analytics, this program applies persuasion and communication to trade policy, digital commerce governance, and economic negotiation. It prepares graduates to manage high-stakes communication in global markets, multinational companies, and regulatory bodies.

Related courses

  • Seminar in International Commerce 1: International Commercial Negotiation Strategy
  • Seminar in International Commerce 1: Foreign Direct Investment and Digital Trade
  • Topics in International Commerce: Digital Trade Rules and Challenges

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: Tuition for the 2 years: CNY 380,000 (the value of the waiver).

Beijing Government Scholarship (BGS) Full package

Source
Beijing Municipal Government, through Peking University.
Tuition covered
100% for the 2-year program (worth CNY 380,000).
Monthly stipend
Type A only: CNY 3,000 (about NT$ 13,410; CNY 1 = NT$ 4.47, Oct 2026), plus free housing and medical insurance. Type B: tuition, housing and insurance, no stipend. Type C: tuition and insurance only.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Competitive. The second year depends on a yearly performance review. Type C holders can apply to move up to A or B.
Separate application?
Yes. Apply in the PKU portal after you apply for self-funded admission.
Who can apply
Non-Chinese citizens under 35 with a degree from a QS Top 500 university. GPA 3.6 or higher for Type A (3.0 for Type B and C). TOEFL iBT 85 or IELTS 6.5. No other government scholarship at the same time.

Source: PKU scholarship and admissions information

4Funding

Global Korea Scholarship, Graduate, University Track (GKS-G) Full package

Source
Korean Government, Ministry of Education, run by NIIED.
Tuition covered
100% for the degree (and a 1-year Korean language program if needed). NIIED pays up to KRW 5,000,000 per semester and Yonsei pays the rest and the admission fee.
Monthly stipend
KRW 1,380,000 (about NT$ 31,947; KRW 1,000 = NT$ 23.15 on 29 Oct 2026). Also a round-trip economy flight, language training, full health insurance and TOPIK level 5/6 grants.
Guaranteed or competitive?
Very competitive. About 1,200 scholars worldwide through the University Track.
Separate application?
Yes. Send the official NIIED forms to Yonsei by post or in person.
Who can apply
Non-Korean citizens (you and your parents) with an accredited bachelor's degree and a CGPA of at least 2.64/4.0 (or top 20%). You cannot apply through the Embassy Track at the same time.

Source: 2026 GKS NIIED guidelines (PDF)
