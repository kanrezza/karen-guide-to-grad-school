4Funding

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS-G) — University Track Full package

Funded by Korean Government / Ministry of Education, administered by NIIED.

Tuition support
  • 100% tuition waiver for up to 2 years.
  • An additional 1-year Korean language programme, if required.
Monthly stipend
Approximately NT$ 31,950/month.
Other benefits
  • Round-trip economy airfare and language training support.
  • Medical insurance and TOPIK Level 5/6 grants.
Who can apply
  • Applicant and both parents must be non-Korean; accredited bachelor’s degree required.
  • Minimum CGPA: 2.64/4.0, 2.80/4.3, 2.91/4.5 or 3.23/5.0; alternatively 80/100 or top 20% of the class.
How to apply
Separate application required: submit official NIIED forms directly to Yonsei by post or in person.
Selection and renewal
Highly competitive; maintain satisfactory academic progress.

The source gives 2026 application dates; it does not provide a 2027 deadline.

4Funding

Beijing Government Scholarship (BGS) — Type A Full package

Funded by Beijing Municipal Government, administered through Peking University.

Tuition support
  • 100% tuition waiver for the 2-year programme.
  • Total tuition value: NT$ 1,698,600.
Monthly stipend
NT$ 13,410/month.
Other benefits
  • Free housing.
  • Medical insurance.
Who can apply
  • Non-Chinese citizens under 35 with a QS Top 500 degree.
  • Type A: GPA at least 3.6/4.0 or 85/100.
  • Scholarship English threshold: TOEFL iBT 85+ or IELTS 6.5+; programme admission requires the higher scores in the checklist.
  • No concurrent government scholarships.
How to apply
  • Apply for self-funded admission first.
  • Submit a separate BGS application through the PKU portal.
Selection and renewal
  • Competitive; second-year funding depends on annual performance review.
  • Current BGS Type C holders may apply to upgrade to Type A or B.

Content source: project team’s Asia research document.
