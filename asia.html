4Funding

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS-G) — University Track Full package*

Funded by Korean Government · NIIED

Tuition support
  • 100% tuition under an awarded GKS scholarship, for up to 2 degree years.
  • NIIED support: up to NT$ 115,750/semester; the university covers the admission fee and approved tuition balance.
Degree living allowance
Approx. NT$ 31,947/month, equivalent to NT$ 383,364/year under 2026 rules.
Language-year support
  • Up to 1 Korean-language year, if required.
  • Approx. NT$ 27,780/month during language training under 2026 rules.
Other benefits
  • Eligible economy airfare and medical insurance under GKS rules.
  • Budget housing and personal costs from the allowance.
Who can apply
  • Applicant and parents must be non-Korean; invited-country quotas apply.
  • Bachelor’s degree and GKS grade requirements must be met.
  • 2026 rules: under 40, with restrictions on previous Korean study or government-funded degrees.
How to apply
  • Submit GKS forms to Yonsei Graduate School by post or in person.
  • The 2026 round also required an online survey; follow the 2027 instructions.
Selection and renewal
  • Competitive Yonsei nomination and NIIED selection.
  • Meet academic and Korean-language requirements throughout the award.

Sources: 2026 NIIED GKS guidelines, Yonsei GKS requirements, Yonsei scholarship overview.

A two-year degree in public administration, comparative policy and international cooperation.

  • Public Policy Analysis
  • Public Economics
  • Strategy and Management of Public and Non-profit Organization

4Funding

Cost without a scholarship: NT$ 849,300/year; NT$ 1,698,600 for 2 years.

Chinese Government Scholarship — Type B Partial

Funded by Chinese Government · China Scholarship Council (CSC).

Tuition support
  • Partial tuition waiver under the 2027 IMPA notice.
  • Waiver amount and remaining tuition are not published.
Monthly stipend
  • NT$ 13,410/month: standard CSC master’s allowance.
  • Confirm the amount and duration in the final award.
Other benefits
Accommodation and medical insurance under the award rules.
Who can apply
  • Non-Chinese bachelor’s graduates; CSC master’s applicants must be under 35.
  • Meet the programme’s work-experience and English requirements.
  • No concurrent Chinese government or PKU scholarship, except permitted one-time awards.
How to apply
  • Apply for the programme by 21 February 2027.
  • Complete the PKU scholarship and CSC applications; PKU agency code: 10001, Type B.
  • Check the separate 2027 scholarship-form deadlines.
Selection and renewal
  • Competitive university recommendation and CSC selection.
  • Annual review required; award terms set the funded period.

Sources: 2027 IMPA admission and partial-tuition award, PKU CSC programme, 2027–28 CSC standard benefits.

BGS is restricted to regular-fee programmes: PKU BGS eligibility. The 2027 IMPA notice lists CSC Type B.

4Funding

Graduate Scholarship for Foreign Students (GSFS) Partial

Funded by Seoul National University.

Tuition support
100% tuition and admission-fee waiver for up to 4 semesters.
Monthly stipend
  • At least NT$ 11,575/month.
  • Spring 2027 master’s standard: 1 year.
  • Amount and duration vary by school; year-two living support is not guaranteed.
Who can apply
  • Applicant and both parents must be non-Korean.
  • All formal education through the bachelor’s degree must be outside Korea.
  • Short exchanges in Korea are allowed; a Korean degree is disqualifying.
  • Apply to a participating school. GSIS had 1 place for Spring 2027.
Selection priority
Priority for nationals of non-advanced economies; Chinese and Japanese applicants are not categorically excluded under the Spring 2027 guide.
How to apply
  • Select GSFS during SNU’s online admission application.
  • Confirm the participating schools and conditions for the next intake.
Selection and renewal
  • Competitive college/school selection.
  • Maintain at least 3.0/4.3 each semester.

Sources: GSFS Spring 2027 guide, Spring 2027 call. Fall 2027 terms are not yet confirmed.
