01 / Your application guide

Words to Know

A few useful terms before you open the program profiles.

Understand the funding

Full package
Full tuition coverage plus a monthly stipend through the named scholarship. Check what you still pay yourself.
Partly funded
Some support is available, but tuition or living costs remain. A full tuition waiver alone belongs here.
Tuition waiver / stipend
A waiver reduces tuition. A stipend is money paid regularly for expenses such as food, rent and transport.
Competitive scholarship
Eligible applicants are assessed for a limited number of awards. Admission does not guarantee funding.

Read the requirements

Separate application
Admission and the scholarship need different forms or submissions. Their deadlines may also differ.
ECTS
The European credit system. A full-time academic year is usually 60 ECTS; subject-credit requirements still need checking.
CEFR
A language scale from A1 to C2. The program decides which tests and scores demonstrate its required level.
EMI certificate
An official letter showing your degree was taught in English. Ask whether the program accepts it instead of a test.

Know your documents

Transcript / degree certificate
Your transcript lists courses and grades. Your degree certificate proves that you completed the degree.
Expected-graduation certificate
A university letter stating when you expect to graduate. Check if it is accepted and when the final degree is due.
Statement of Purpose / research proposal
The statement explains your fit and goals. A proposal sets out a research question and how you plan to study it.
Proof of funds
Evidence of available money, usually a bank statement or accepted scholarship letter. The amount and format vary.
Scholarship abbreviations you will see in this guide
MOE / MOFA / TaiwanICDF
Taiwan's Ministry of Education, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and International Cooperation and Development Fund. Each has its own eligibility rules.
GKS / GSFS / BGS
Global Korea Scholarship, SNU's Graduate Scholarship for Excellent Foreign Students, and Beijing Government Scholarship.
Erasmus Mundus
Joint master's programs delivered by university partnerships, with competitive EU-funded scholarships on eligible routes.
GAP
Graduate Assistantship Programme: teaching or other assigned duties attached to some research scholarships. It is not a separate scholarship.

03 / Your application guide

Timeline for 2027 entry

Use this as a planning map, then check the official call for your chosen intake.

  1. Nov–Dec 2026

    PREPARE & APPLY

    Get your first applications ready.

    • Our profiles list NUS: 1 Nov, NTU: 15 Nov and PKU: 25 Dec. Check the intended cycle.
    • NTUT and the TaiwanICDF scholarship window are listed as opening in December.
    • Request English records, language evidence and references before the earliest deadline.

  2. January 2027

    EUROPEAN CALLS

    Finish your program-specific work.

    • Profile dates: Mundus Journalism: 10 Jan; Gothenburg and Lund: 15 Jan; Jyväskylä: 21 Jan.
    • Check the deadline's local time and the separate dates for documents or fee payment.
    • Upload work samples, motivation letters or videos in the requested format.

  3. February 2027

    FUNDING STEPS

    Give scholarships their own calendar.

    • MOE and MOFA Taiwan Scholarship applications usually begin around February. Confirm your overseas mission's dates and eligibility.
    • Check separate scholarship instructions for Lund and Gothenburg.
    • Gothenburg's profile lists 1 Feb for fee payment and supporting documents.

  4. March 2027

    CHECK THE DETAILS

    Keep admission and funding dates apart.

    • CLIDE: 7 Mar reported; NCCU: about 9 Mar estimated; NTUT: 10 Mar in the profiles.
    • 15 Mar is the listed ICDF scholarship deadline. Confirm SAC's program participation and separate admission date.
    • Check your overseas mission's closing dates for MOE and MOFA awards.

  5. Apr–Jun 2027

    COMPARE OFFERS

    Read the offer and the funding letter.

    • Track results and any interviews or requests for additional documents.
    • MCU's fall profile lists 31 May. Confirm the current admission call.
    • Compare tuition, stipend duration, renewal conditions and your remaining costs before accepting.

  6. Jul–Sep 2027

    GET READY TO GO

    Complete the steps after your offer.

    • Submit your final degree and any verified documents by the university's deadline.
    • Arrange your visa or residence permission, housing, insurance and travel as required.
    • Check orientation, registration and arrival dates; the start date differs by program.

Check the intake, not just the year. STUST's listed 30 November 2026 deadline is for a February 2027 start, which may be before you graduate. The SNU and Yonsei calls currently listed in the guide have closed. Use their official sites to find the next applicable round.

04 / Your application guide

Translations & authentication

Make sure your documents are accepted before paying for extra processing.

1

Ask YZU first

Request official English versions of your transcript and degree or expected-graduation certificate. Use the same name as on your passport.

2

Read the exact rules

Check accepted languages, original or copy requirements, and who may certify a translation. A university-issued English document may remove the need to translate it.

3

Confirm verification

Authentication checks a document's official origin. Follow the receiving university's instructions for the country that issued your documents, including any legalization or apostille route.

4

Keep a complete set

Save clear scans of the original, translation and certification together. Check whether verification is due at application, after an offer or at registration.

Translation and authentication are different. A certified or notarized translation follows the institution's rules for an accepted translation. It does not automatically replace verification of the original academic document. If instructions are unclear, email admissions and keep the written reply.
