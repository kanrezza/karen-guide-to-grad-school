01 / The essentials

Words to Know

The basics, in plain English.

Funding

Full package
Full tuition + monthly stipend. Check any remaining costs.
Partly funded
Some costs covered; you pay the rest.
Tuition waiver / stipend
Waiver: reduced tuition. Stipend: regular living allowance.
Competitive scholarship
Limited awards. Admission does not guarantee funding.

Requirements

Separate application
A separate scholarship form, often with its own deadline.
ECTS
European credits. Usually 60 ECTS per study year.
CEFR
Language levels from A1 to C2. Required scores vary.
EMI certificate
Proof of English-taught study. Test waivers depend on the program.

Documents

Transcript / degree certificate
Transcript: courses and grades. Degree certificate: proof of graduation.
Expected-graduation certificate
Your planned graduation date. Check whether the program accepts it.
Statement of Purpose / research proposal
Statement: your fit and goals. Proposal: your research plan.
Proof of funds
Bank statement or accepted scholarship letter. Required amounts vary.
Scholarship abbreviations
MOE / MOFA / TaiwanICDF
Taiwan education, foreign affairs and development scholarships. Nationality rules vary.
GKS / GSFS / CSC
Global Korea Scholarship; SNU’s foreign-student scholarship; Chinese Government Scholarship.
Erasmus Mundus
Joint master's programs with competitive EU-funded scholarships.
GAP
Graduate Assistantship Programme: duties linked to a research scholarship.

03 / The essentials

Timeline for 2027 entry

What to do each month. Check each university’s website for the latest dates.

  1. Nov–Dec 2026

    START APPLYING

    Prepare and send early applications.

    • Apply by 15 Nov for NTU or 1 Dec for NUS Economics.
    • NTUT and TaiwanICDF scholarship applications open in December.
    • Request transcripts, language-test results and recommendation letters.

  2. January 2027

    SUBMIT APPLICATIONS

    Meet the European program deadlines.

    • Mundus Journalism: apply by 10 Jan.
    • Gothenburg and Lund: apply by 15 Jan.
    • Jyväskylä: apply by 21 Jan.

  3. February 2027

    APPLY FOR FUNDING

    Check the separate scholarship steps.

    • MOE / MOFA Taiwan Scholarships usually open now. Ask the Taiwan office in your home country for dates.
    • Check the scholarship forms for Lund and Gothenburg.
    • For Gothenburg, pay the application fee and upload documents by 1 Feb.
    • PKU IMPA-CPPIC: apply by 21 Feb for scholarship consideration.

  4. March 2027

    FINISH SUBMISSIONS

    Check university and scholarship deadlines.

    • CLIDE: apply by 7 Mar (date unconfirmed).
    • NCCU: apply by 4 Mar.
    • NTUT: apply by 10 Mar.
    • TaiwanICDF: apply by 15 Mar. Confirm SAC eligibility and its separate admission date.
    • MOE / MOFA: ask your local Taiwan office for closing dates.

  5. Apr–Jun 2027

    CHOOSE A PROGRAM

    Check results and compare offers.

    • Check your email for results, interviews or missing documents.
    • Still applying? MCU lists 31 May as its fall application deadline.
    • Before accepting, check what you must pay and how to keep the scholarship.

  6. Jul–Sep 2027

    PREPARE TO START

    Complete the steps after acceptance.

    • Send your final graduation certificate and any required authenticated documents.
    • Arrange your visa, housing, insurance and travel.
    • Confirm when to arrive, register and attend orientation.

Check pending calls: SNU’s Spring 2027 round has closed. Fall 2027 dates for SNU and STUST, and the Yonsei GKS 2027 call, are pending. Check your graduation date before applying.

04 / The essentials

Translations & authentication

Check the rules before paying for extra processing.

1

Request English copies

Ask YZU for English records. Match your passport name.

2

Check translation rules

Confirm accepted languages and who can certify translations.

3

Check authentication

Follow the university’s verification rules for Taiwan-issued documents.

4

Save the full set

Keep originals, translations and certificates. Check when verification is due.

Translation ≠ authentication. One changes language; the other verifies origin. Unsure? Ask admissions and save the reply.
