Funding notes and sources

Read before applying

  • Full packages cover tuition and a stipend. All awards remain competitive.
  • TNUA’s 2027 ICDF program list and MCU’s 2027–28 tuition top-up are pending.
  • NCCU excludes credit fees. NTUT covers 50% tuition.
  • Yonsei and SNU show closed reference rounds; no new deadline is assumed.

Costs and dates

  • SEK900 fees remain in SEK: the research files supply no NT$ rate.
  • Aarhus’s general fee of about NT$5,411 is not confirmed for the separate Mundus process.
  • Unknown fees are not treated as free. CLIDE’s 7 March date is reported and needs confirmation.
